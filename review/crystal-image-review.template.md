Visual agreement with a majority is corroborating evidence, **not scientific proof**. Prefer museum, mineralogical or gemological identification over an unlabeled marketplace listing. A repeated mislabeled stock photo is not three independent confirmations. Distinguish similarly named commercial stones (for example, rainbow moonstone/labradorite versus orthoclase moonstone). Do not alter a photo to manufacture a diagnostic effect. A familiar-looking image without permission to reuse it is not an acceptable final selection.

Store client-specific records under ignored `review/runs/`. An existing repository image or earlier sample is not evidence that the 3–4-image review was performed for the present job. If a checked, reusable research record already exists, inspect and verify its candidates and document that reuse; do not silently assert a fresh search happened.
